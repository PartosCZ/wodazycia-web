Kids

We want to see a young generation that lives with God! That’s why, as volunteers, we create a place where in a friendly atmosphere and in creative ways we talk about God, play, pray, worship and even have breakfast together!

Sunday with kids

Every week we run a programme for children aged 4–9
Check-in is open 10:50 – 11:15

For parents and toddlers up to the age of 3 there is a play room and a changing table. The room has a TV showing the service.
